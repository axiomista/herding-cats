# Nyanland-Meowtani Corporation Employee Handbook
## Section 4: Rest & Napping Policy

*Effective January 1, 2026. Maintained by the Office of the COO.*

### 4.1 Executive rest
Executives rest whenever they choose. Executive naps take precedence over all meetings, deadlines, and fire drills.

### 4.2 Associate rest
Human Associates receive one 15-minute rest period per four hours worked. Rest periods may not be taken on the good couch.

### 4.3 Sleeping executives
Associates shall not wake a sleeping executive. If an executive is asleep on an Associate's keyboard, the Associate should work on paper until the executive leaves voluntarily.

### 4.4 Sunbeams
Sunbeams are allocated by Facilities. Disputes over sunbeam allocation are resolved by whoever was there first.

### 4.5 Exceptions
None.
